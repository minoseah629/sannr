# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in full. The short version: be respectful, assume good faith, no
harassment of any kind, and disagree about the work rather than about
each other.

Reports of unacceptable behavior go to **conduct@sannr.dev**.
Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers, who will respond
within three business days.

Enforcement follows the Covenant's guidelines: correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban — in that order, proportionate to the
behavior.
